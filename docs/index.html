        Where it started

        Late on November 3, 2026, as the midterm results came in, Trump posted a photo of a red cap with the single word “VETO” on Truth Social. Days earlier he had said he would become “Mr. Veto” if Democrats won Congress. This counter starts the next day. See the post in the archive.

        How the count works: a veto is counted when Congress.gov marks a bill as vetoed by the President (pocket vetoes included), or when the White House announces a veto of a bill. A veto threat is counted when it comes from Trump’s own posts, or from an official White House statement that recommends a veto. Headlines are shown separately and don’t change the count. A script checks these sources once a day, so a new entry can take up to a day to appear.

        Sources:

        • Congress.gov, the official source for bills and their latest actions
        • White House announcements of vetoed bills
        • Trump’s Truth, an independent archive of Truth Social posts
        • Headlines from major news outlets, linked individually
