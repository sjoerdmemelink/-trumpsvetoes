        How the count works: a veto is counted when it appears on the U.S. Senate’s official list of presidential vetoes, which includes pocket vetoes. A veto threat is counted when it comes from Trump’s own posts, or from an official White House statement that recommends a veto. Headlines are shown separately and don’t change the count. A script checks these sources once a day, so a new entry can take up to a day to appear.
